=== VIP THEATER LOCAL VIDEO PLAYLIST ===

You can play any video from your computer in the VIP Theater in two ways:

1. BROWSE FROM ANYWHERE ON YOUR PC (Recommended):
   - While seated on the VIP Theater Sofa, press [O] or click [📁 BROWSE LOCAL VIDEO FILE].
   - Select any downloaded video from your computer (Downloads, Videos, Desktop, etc.).
   - The video will instantly load and project onto the giant IMAX screen with Dolby Atmos spatial audio!

2. DRAG & DROP INTO THIS FOLDER:
   - Simply copy or move your video files (.mp4, .webm, .mov, .m4v, .avi, .mkv) directly into:
     Assets/StreamingAssets/Videos/
   - The theater automatically scans this folder when launched!

HOTKEYS (While Seated on VIP Theater Sofa):
  [O]              - Browse PC Video File
  [SPACE]          - Play / Pause
  [S]              - Stop Playback
  [LEFT ARROW]     - Seek Backward 10 Seconds
  [RIGHT ARROW]    - Seek Forward 10 Seconds
  [UP ARROW]       - Volume Up
  [DOWN ARROW]     - Volume Down
  [M]              - Mute / Unmute
  [ESC]            - Exit Theater Mode (Stands up and restores PC workstation)
